# Install InsuroX Prime as an app on your phone

InsuroX works like a normal app once it is on your Home Screen: it opens full screen, with its own icon, and loads faster on repeat visits.

**Open this link first, in your phone's browser:** https://admin.kempanna.com/login

You need an internet connection to sign in and to see your latest data.

---

## Android

### Chrome (recommended)
1. Open https://admin.kempanna.com/login in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu (top right).
3. Tap **Install app** (on some phones it says **Add to Home screen**).
4. Tap **Install**.

An **Install InsuroX** card may also appear inside the app. Tap **Install** on it.

### Samsung Internet
1. Open the link in **Samsung Internet**.
2. Tap the **☰** menu (bottom right).
3. Tap **Add page to**, then **Home screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

### Microsoft Edge
1. Open the link in **Edge**.
2. Tap the **⋯** menu (bottom centre).
3. Tap **Add to phone** (or **Add to Home screen**).
4. Tap **Add**.

### Firefox
1. Open the link in **Firefox**.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu.
3. Tap **Install**.
4. Tap **Add**.

---

## iPhone and iPad (iOS / iPadOS)

Apple does not show an install button, so you add it by hand from the **Share** menu.

### Safari (recommended)
1. Open https://admin.kempanna.com/login in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button, the square with an arrow pointing up (bottom of the screen on iPhone, top right on iPad).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** (top right).

### Chrome on iPhone (iOS 16.4 or newer)
1. Open the link in **Chrome**.
2. Tap the **Share** button in the address bar (top right).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

### Edge on iPhone (iOS 16.4 or newer)
1. Open the link in **Edge**.
2. Tap the **⋯** menu (bottom centre), then **Share**.
3. Tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

> If you don't see **Add to Home Screen**, scroll to the bottom of the Share sheet and tap **Edit Actions**, then add it. On older iPhones (before iOS 16.4) use **Safari** only.

---

## After installing

- Open the **InsuroX** icon on your Home Screen.
- **Sign in once inside the installed app.** On iPhone it keeps its own sign-in, separate from Safari.
- You will stay signed in on that phone.

## Update the app

Nothing to do: close and reopen it, and you get the latest version.

## Remove the app

- **Android:** press and hold the icon, then tap **Uninstall** (or **Remove**).
- **iPhone / iPad:** press and hold the icon, then tap **Remove App** > **Delete App**.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
|---|---|
| No **Install** option on Android | Use **Chrome**, open the link (not a bookmark of another page), and refresh once. |
| No **Add to Home Screen** on iPhone | Open the link in **Safari**, not inside another app (WhatsApp, Gmail and similar). |
| The page looks zoomed in or cut off on the right (iPhone) | Pinch out with two fingers, or close and reopen the app. |
| "You're offline" bar | Check your mobile data or Wi-Fi. The app needs a connection to load your data. |
| Still stuck | Send a screenshot and your phone model to your agency admin. |
